FAQ

Questions, answered

Does filling out a form cost anything?+
No. Every tool on The Car Scout is free to use. You only ever pay if you choose to move forward on a deal.
Will applying hurt my credit?+
Getting started is a soft inquiry and won't affect your score. A hard inquiry only happens with your permission once you're ready to move forward.
What happens after I submit?+
Your info comes straight to us and a real person follows up quickly — by phone, text, or email, whichever you prefer.
Is my information secure?+
Yes. Your information is sent over an encrypted connection and is only used to help with your request. We never sell your data.
Do you buy cars with a loan still on them?+
Yes. Just tell us your payoff amount on the Sell My Car form and we'll handle the rest.
